Who we are

Guiding students towards a more confident future.

Bihar State Educational Development and Research Council is committed to finding hidden talent and supporting children and young people with the recognition and resources needed to continue their education.

Founded by Bibhishan Kumar, the council brings together examinations, awards and community support for learners across Bihar.
